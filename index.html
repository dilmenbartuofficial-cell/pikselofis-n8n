PikselOfis n8n

PikselOfis n8n is a private, single-user automation tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and connects to the owner's own Google Drive, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts to read statistics of the owner's own videos and, only after the owner's manual approval, to upload the owner's own content.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
