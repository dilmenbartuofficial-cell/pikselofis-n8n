Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

PikselOfis n8n is a personal tool used only by its owner on the owner's own computer.

  • Data accessed: only the owner's own Google Drive files created by the tool, and public statistics (views, likes, comments) and uploads of the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • Storage: data and access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • Use of Google user data: complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local data is deleted with the tool.
